# Ashen Hollow – prompts for the other six classes (same rules as the Warrior file)
Every prompt ends with the style anchor and the clean-mesh line from warrior_prompts.md.
Shared motif: the Ashen Hollow crest (a hollow tree inside a ring with a small ember at its heart) appears once per set.

## Rogue — dark violet, black leather, silver, a thin ember line
Weapons: 1) Ashfang Daggers — "a pair of curved fantasy daggers, short reverse-curved blackened steel blades with a thin ember-orange edge line, wrapped violet-black leather grips, small ring pommels" 2) Veilcutter — "a slim one-edged fantasy short sword, dark steel blade with a hooked tip, silver crossguard shaped like a crescent moon" 3) Throwing star — "a four-pointed fantasy throwing star, dark steel with the Ashen Hollow crest punched through the centre".
Hard pieces: Mask-cowl clasp — "a small silver half-mask covering nose and mouth, fine filigree"; Bracer — "a single fitted leather bracer with three silver buckles and a hidden blade slot"; Belt — "a slim leather belt with many small pouches and a silver crest buckle".
Meshy paint: "stylized hand-painted RPG textures, black and deep-violet leather, dull silver metal, a faint ember-orange line glow, soft painted highlights".

## Ranger — forest green, warm brown leather, bone and feathers

Hard pieces: Quiver — "a leather quiver with fletched arrows, crest stamped on the side"; Pauldron — "a single layered leather shoulder guard with a small bone plate"; Bracer — "a single archer's leather bracer with feathers tied on".
Meshy paint: "stylized hand-painted RPG textures, forest-green cloth, warm brown leather, pale bone, soft painted highlights".

## Shaman — sea teal, bone, beads, carved wood
